Privacy Policy — Scenera

Last updated: October 2026 (comments, lists, soundtracks, analytics, purchases and additional services added)

Scenera ("the App") is a TV show tracking application. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password, or your Google account sign-in (we receive your email address and name from Google), used to create and secure your account. You may optionally add a display name and a profile photo.
  • Watch history: the shows and episodes you mark as watched, planned, watching, completed, dropped, or favorited, along with any ratings or reactions you provide.
  • User-generated content: notes, reactions, and similar content you create within the App and attach to shows or episodes.
  • Comments (optional): comments you post on an episode or movie, including a picture or GIF you attach, are stored on our servers and are visible to other Scenera users. Comments you choose to post to the shared Community board are also sent to CommsUni (comments by CommsUni.tv), which shows them in other apps as well; they are identified only by an anonymous ID derived from your account, and you can delete your own Community comments in the App.
  • Lists and saved soundtracks (optional): lists you create (which you can make public and share by link) and songs you save from a show's or movie's soundtrack. Searches for soundtracks may be logged on our server to improve matching.
  • Username (optional): a public identifier you can set so other users can find and add you as a friend without sharing your email address.
  • Direct messages (optional): if you connect with another user as a friend, messages you send each other within the App are stored on our servers to enable that conversation. Only the sender and recipient can see message content.
  • Imported data (optional): if you choose to import a TV Time (or similar) export file, we process the show titles, episode counts, and ratings contained in that file to populate your watchlist. The uploaded file itself is processed to build your library and is not retained beyond what's needed to complete the import.
  • Usage and device data: basic usage events (for example that the App was opened or a show was added), and, when you sign up, the source that brought you (such as a store listing or link campaign), your language, time zone and platform. Google Firebase Analytics also collects app usage and diagnostic data, including a device or installation identifier.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to Pro, Google Play and RevenueCat process the purchase. We receive your subscription status and purchase history, not your payment card details.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, your device registers a token used to deliver reminders (e.g. new episodes, upcoming premieres) to that device.
  • Crash and error data: if the App crashes or encounters an error, technical details about that event (such as the device type, app version, and the error itself) are automatically collected to help us fix bugs.
  • AI chat messages (optional): if you use the "Ask Scenera AI" feature, the message you type is sent, together with a summary of your watch history (show titles you've completed or are watching), to our AI provider in order to generate a personalized recommendation. See "Third-party services" below.
  • Advertising data: the App shows ads, served through Google AdMob, to free-tier users (Pro subscribers do not see ads). AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and other technical data to serve and measure ads, and — depending on the ad consent choice you make when prompted — to personalize them. See "Third-party services" below.

How we use this information

Your data is used to provide the App's core functionality: tracking what you watch, showing upcoming episodes, generating AI-powered recommendations when you ask for them, personalizing suggestions (for example, "Top shows for you" on the Explore screen is based on the genres of shows on your lists), sending optional notifications, and syncing your library across devices. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Free-tier use of the App does involve advertising, served through Google AdMob as described above and in "Third-party services" below; Pro subscribers do not see ads and are not served ad-related data through AdMob for that purpose.

Sharing between users (Friends feature)

If you choose to connect with another Scenera user as a friend within the App, the shows you've marked as "Favorite" become visible to that connected friend, and vice versa. Connected friends can also send each other direct messages within the App; message content is stored on our servers to enable this feature and is visible only to the sender and recipient. You can also send a friend a card for a specific show, episode or movie, with an optional message. No other part of your watchlist or watch history is shared with friends. Disconnecting a friend (or either party deleting their account) permanently deletes any messages exchanged between you.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each processes only the data described below, for the purpose stated.

  • Supabase — hosts our database, handles account authentication, and stores uploaded profile photos. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — we use TMDB's API to retrieve show information, images, and cast details. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. See TMDB's privacy policy.
  • Sentry — receives crash and error data (see "Crash and error data" above) so we can diagnose and fix problems in the App. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Analytics (Google) — deliver push notifications to your device using your device's notification token, and provide app usage and diagnostic statistics. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages Pro subscriptions together with Google Play, using an account identifier. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In — lets you create an account or sign in with your Google account. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — checks at sign-up that the request comes from a person and not a bot, and receives technical information about your browser or device for that purpose. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • CommsUni — operates the shared Community comments board described above. See commsuni.tv.
  • GIPHY — provides GIFs for comments. When you search for a GIF, the search term is sent to GIPHY by our server. See GIPHY's privacy policy.
  • Deezer and Apple (iTunes Search) — used to find songs for the soundtracks feature; the title of the show or movie and the song details are sent to look them up. See Deezer's and Apple's privacy policies.
  • Vercel — hosts the Scenera website and web app and provides web usage statistics. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Groq — powers the "Ask Scenera AI" feature. When you use this feature, your message and a summary of your watch history are sent to Groq to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use the AI chat feature. See Groq's privacy policy.
  • Resend — sends account-related emails, such as email confirmation and password reset messages, to the email address on your account. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads to free-tier users. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data to serve, measure, and (depending on your consent choice) personalize ads. When required (e.g. in the EEA/UK), the App asks for your consent before AdMob personalizes ads, through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can decline personalization and still see (non-personalized) ads. Pro subscribers do not see ads. See Google's privacy policy and AdMob's data disclosure.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, in either of two ways:

  • In the App: Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your data immediately and automatically.
  • On the web: visit our account deletion page and follow the instructions there — useful if you no longer have the App installed.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account credentials, display name, username and profile photo, watchlist, watch history, ratings, reactions, private notes, lists and saved soundtracks, comments you wrote (and pictures attached to them), friend connections, any messages you've exchanged with friends, your push notification token, usage events, and any data imported from TV Time or similar sources. Some data is held by others under their own policies and is not removed by us: purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat, crash reports kept by Sentry for a limited period, and comments you posted to the CommsUni Community board (delete those in the App first). If you'd like to keep a copy of your data first, you can export it from the Profile screen before deleting your account. Requests submitted via the web page are fulfilled within 30 days.

Your choices and controls

  • Profile photo, notifications, and the AI chat feature are all optional — the App's core tracking functionality works without them.
  • Where required, you can choose whether AdMob personalizes the ads you see (or opt out of personalization) via the consent prompt shown in the App. Subscribing to Pro removes ads entirely.
  • You can export a full copy of your watchlist and watch history at any time from the Profile screen.
  • You can disconnect a friend at any time to stop sharing your favorites with them; this also deletes any messages exchanged between you.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, as described above.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
